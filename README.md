# Retail-Sales-Analysis-Using-Power-BI

📊 Online Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard

1️⃣ Project Introduction

Hello everyone, my name is Deepak Kumar, and today I will present my Online Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard, which I built using Microsoft Power BI.

The main objective of this project is to analyze retail transaction data and transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights that help organizations understand their customers, products, and revenue performance.

2️⃣ Dashboard Overview

(Point to the dashboard while explaining)

This dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of the retail business performance through several key metrics and visualizations.

It helps stakeholders quickly monitor:

Overall sales performance

Customer activity

Product performance

Country-wise revenue distribution

Monthly revenue trends

The dashboard is also interactive, allowing users to filter data by:

Country

Date Range

This allows decision-makers to analyze specific time periods and geographic regions.

3️⃣ Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

At the top of the dashboard, we have three main KPI indicators:

🧑 Total Customers – 4.37K

This metric shows the total number of unique customers who made purchases.

Monitoring this KPI helps businesses understand customer base size and engagement.

📦 Total Products – 3.68K

This KPI represents the total number of unique products sold in the dataset.

It helps track product diversity and inventory performance.

💰 Total Revenue – 8300K

This metric shows the total revenue generated from all transactions.

It is the most important business metric and provides a clear view of overall financial performance.

4️⃣ Revenue Trend Analysis (Monthly)

(Point to the line chart)

This chart displays Total Revenue by Month.

It helps identify seasonal trends and sales patterns throughout the year.

Key insights include:

Revenue steadily increases in the last quarter of the year.

The highest revenue was recorded in November at 1.13M.

This suggests holiday season demand significantly boosts sales.

Such insights help companies prepare inventory and marketing strategies for peak seasons.

5️⃣ Revenue & Quantity by Top Countries

(Explain the horizontal bar chart)

This visualization shows the Top 10 countries contributing to revenue and quantity sold.

Key observations:

The United Kingdom dominates sales, generating approximately 6.7M revenue.

Other major markets include:

Netherlands

Ireland

Germany

France

This indicates that the company has a strong customer base in Europe, especially in the UK market.

6️⃣ Top Customers by Revenue

(Explain the bar chart)

This chart highlights the top customers based on revenue contribution.

Insights:

Some customers contribute significantly higher revenue compared to others.

The highest contributing customer generated approximately 279K in sales.

This analysis helps businesses identify high-value customers, which is useful for:

Loyalty programs

Personalized marketing

Customer retention strategies

7️⃣ Top Products by Revenue

(Explain product chart)

This visualization shows the top-performing products based on revenue.

Key insights:

Product 22423 generated the highest revenue at 133K.

Other products such as 85123 and 85099 also performed strongly.

Businesses can use this information to:

Promote best-selling products

Optimize inventory planning

Identify high-demand items

8️⃣ Global Revenue Distribution

(Explain the map)

The map visualization shows revenue distribution across different countries worldwide.

Key observations:

The majority of revenue comes from European countries.

There are also contributions from regions like North America and Asia.

This helps organizations understand global market reach and expansion opportunities.

9️⃣ Interactive Filters

(Point to slicers)

The dashboard includes interactive filters such as:

Country filter

Date range selector

These filters allow users to drill down into specific markets and time periods, making the dashboard flexible and user-friendly for business analysis.

🔟 Business Value of This Dashboard

This dashboard helps organizations:

Track sales performance in real time

Identify top customers and products

Analyze geographic revenue distribution

Detect seasonal sales trends

Support data-driven decision making
