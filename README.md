# Retail-Sales-Analysis-Using-Power-BI

📊 Online Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard

1️⃣ Project Introduction

Hello everyone, my name is Zayn Malik, and today I will present my Online Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard, which I built using Microsoft Power BI.

The main objective of this project is to analyze retail transaction data and transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights that help organizations understand their customers, products, and revenue performance.

2️⃣ Dashboard Overview

(Point to the dashboard while explaining)

This dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of the retail business performance through several key metrics and visualizations.

It helps stakeholders quickly monitor:

Overall sales performance

Customer activity

Product performance

Country-wise revenue distribution

Monthly revenue trends

The dashboard is also interactive, allowing users to filter data by:

Country

Date Range

This allows decision-makers to analyze specific time periods and geographic regions.

3️⃣ Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

At the top of the dashboard, we have three main KPI indicators:

🧑 Total Customers – 4.37K

This metric shows the total number of unique customers who made purchases.

Monitoring this KPI helps businesses understand customer base size and engagement.

📦 Total Products – 3.68K

This KPI represents the total number of unique products sold in the dataset.

It helps track product diversity and inventory performance.

💰 Total Revenue – 8300K

This metric shows the total revenue generated from all transactions.

It is the most important business metric and provides a clear view of overall financial performance.

4️⃣ Revenue Trend Analysis (Monthly)

(Point to the line chart)

This chart displays Total Revenue by Month.

It helps identify seasonal trends and sales patterns throughout the year.

Key insights include:

Revenue steadily increases in the last quarter of the year.

The highest revenue was recorded in November at 1.13M.

This suggests holiday season demand significantly boosts sales.

Such insights help companies prepare inventory and marketing strategies for peak seasons.
